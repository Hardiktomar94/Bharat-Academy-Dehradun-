CGL

SSC Combined Graduate Level Examination (SSC CGL) is a national-level competitive examination conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to various Group B and Group C posts in central government ministries, departments, and organizations of India. It is one of the most popular government exams among graduates because it offers stable jobs, good salary, government allowances, promotion opportunities, and job security. Through SSC CGL, candidates can get posts such as Income Tax Inspector, CBI Sub-Inspector, Assistant Section Officer, Auditor, Accountant, Examiner, and many other administrative and investigative roles. Candidates must usually have a bachelor’s degree from a recognized university, while the age limit generally ranges from 18 to 32 years depending on the post, with relaxation for reserved categories. The examination is conducted in multiple tiers including computer-based objective tests, skill tests, and document verification. Subjects mainly include Mathematics, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge, and Statistics for certain posts. SSC CGL is known for its high competition level because lakhs of students appear every year to secure prestigious central government jobs with attractive pay levels and career growth opportunities.

Our mission is to make every student capable of cracking CGL through quality teaching, regular practice, personal mentorship, and smart preparation strategies, even with limited resources. We focus on building confidence, strong concepts, and consistency so that every aspirant can move closer to a successful government career.

NDA

The National Defence Academy (NDA) examination is a national-level defence entrance exam conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for candidates who wish to join the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force after completing their school education. It is one of the most respected and challenging defence examinations in India, attracting lakhs of aspirants every year who dream of serving the nation as officers in the Indian Armed Forces. Through NDA, selected candidates receive military training, academic education, leadership development, and personality enhancement before becoming commissioned officers. Candidates must generally have completed or be appearing in Class 12 from a recognized board, while the age limit is usually between 16.5 and 19.5 years. The selection process includes a written examination followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview, medical examination, and document verification. Subjects in the written exam mainly include Mathematics and General Ability Test (GAT), which covers English, Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography, and Current Affairs. NDA is known for its high competition level, discipline-oriented environment, and the opportunity to build an honourable career filled with pride, adventure, responsibility, and service to the nation.

At Bharat Academy Dehradun, we believe that clearing the National Defence Academy Examination is not about expensive coaching facilities, but about discipline, dedication, and the right mentorship. We focus on building leadership qualities, consistency, and determination so that students from every background can move closer to achieving their dream of serving the nation as future officers in the Indian Armed Forces.

CDS

The Combined Defence Services Examination is a prestigious national-level defence examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for graduates who aspire to join the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force as commissioned officers. CDS offers candidates an opportunity to serve the nation with honour while receiving professional military training, leadership development, and a respected career in the Armed Forces. The selection process includes a written examination followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview and medical examination, where candidates are tested on academic knowledge, intelligence, communication skills, personality, and leadership qualities. Subjects in the written examination mainly include English, General Knowledge, and Mathematics for certain academies. CDS is known for its discipline, competitive environment, career stability, and the pride of wearing the uniform while serving the country.
